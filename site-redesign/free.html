Meet Quamay “i3” Sams

Quamay Sams, E-RYT 500, teaches strength-based yoga built on isometrics and end-range control. He is over a decade into teaching and a student of Jason Crandell, and in that time he has managed studios, led teacher trainings across the country, and taught retreats and intensives internationally, most recently on the Portuguese coast. His teaching lives at the intersection of biomechanics and traditional practice: isometric strength, end-range control, and the nervous system patterns that decide how much of your body you actually have access to.
