How the numbers work
  • Monthly income = Σ amount × frequency factor (weekly 52/12, every two weeks 26/12, twice a month 2, monthly 1, yearly 1/12).
  • Left each month = income − expenses − savings contributions − debt minimums. If this is negative, no timeline can move forward, so it is flagged at the top of the dashboard.
  • Emergency fund (Steps 1 & 3) = balance of savings goals linked to the emergency fund. Step 1 is done at $1,000. Step 3 target = months × (essential expenses + mortgage payment).
  • Step 2 progress = (starting balance − current balance) ÷ starting balance, over all non-mortgage debt.
  • Step 4 target = 15% of total income, per month. Progress = monthly contributions to goals linked to retirement ÷ that target.
  • Timeline engine runs month by month: add interest (balance × APR ÷ 12), pay every minimum, then send what's left over, any extra payment due that month, and the minimums freed by paid-off debts, in Baby Step order: $1,000 emergency fund → debts (avalanche or snowball order) → full emergency fund → mortgage. Savings contributions keep flowing to their goals throughout.
  • Other goals finish in (target − saved) ÷ monthly contribution months. No investment growth is assumed.
  • Debt-to-income = monthly debt minimums ÷ monthly income.
